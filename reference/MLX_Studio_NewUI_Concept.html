What every lesson gives you

Predict first
The coach asks for your guess before it explains, then points you to the next experiment to try.
Ask in your language
Hints in English, हिन्दी or मराठी, and a Listen button that reads them aloud.
Hear the error
Pitch follows the error, so you can hear a run settle, bounce or blow up.
Bring your own data
Type points or upload a CSV in linear regression and K-means, or pick a preset dataset.
